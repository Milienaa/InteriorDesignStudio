01 · Research

What the market and twelve studio websites told us

Market signals

  • Warm minimalism tops the 2026 style forecasts: limewash, light oak, earthy texture. Apartment Therapy
  • Wellness is the top emerging trend (56% of designers), and daylight counts as wellness. Fixr 2026
  • Independent studios do better when they specialise. ASID outlook
  • In Ukraine, design is priced per m²; turnkey renovation runs about $600–1,000/m². novyny.live

Gaps on competitor sites

  • No fee or budget signal on any of the 12 homepages checked.
  • Process hidden. Only one studio lists its steps, and none give durations.
  • 10 of 12 present as multi-sector, so a 70 m² flat owner can't tell if they qualify.
  • Projects show a title only, with no m², scope, duration or budget.
  • The call to action is a passive "Contact", not a booked call.
  • 6 of 11 have no H1 heading; one site has 30 images with empty alt text.

What we take from it

  • Own one specialism the others ignore: light.
  • Publish fees per m² and a process with weeks.
  • Give every project the numbers a client decides on.
  • Make the main action a 30-minute consultation.
  • Build lean and accessible: one H1, real alt text, light images.

Learning note

Niche research is two questions: what do clients want now (market signals), and what are competitors failing to give them (gaps)? A gap that matches a real client need is where a small brand can win.

We audited real studio websites (Norm Architects, Studio KO, Studio McGee, YODEZEEN, balbek bureau and others) instead of guessing.

One promise, for one kind of home

For owners of north-facing, deep-plan and older apartments in Kyiv, Oriel is the interior studio that designs around the light a home actually gets, proven by a light study at 08:00, 13:00 and 18:00, published fees, and five steps with dates.

Learning note

Positioning = audience + promise + proof. The proof matters most: anyone can say "we love light", but a light study at three set hours is something a client can check.

We compared three directions (daylight, restoring old flats, fixed-fee clarity) and chose daylight because no competitor owns it and it is far from the Haven reference. The clarity idea became a rule for every section instead of a separate brand.

Learning note

An oriel is a bay window that projects from an upper floor and catches light from three sides. The symbol is that window drawn in plan, the way an architect would, with the sun above it. One line weight and one dot keep it legible at 16 px.

A quick web check found no interior studio called Oriel (only a London restaurant). A real launch would also need a trademark search.

Learning note

In real daylight, sunlit surfaces look warm and shadows look cool, because shadows are lit by the blue sky. The palette borrows that: warm limewash neutrals plus one cool slate accent. It also avoids a cliché. Cream plus serif plus terracotta is now the most common "AI-made" look, so the accent moved from warm to cool.

Contrast ratio measures how readable text is on its background. WCAG asks for at least 4.5 : 1 for body text and 3 : 1 for form borders and icons. Every pair above was calculated, not eyeballed.

Learning note

Six display serifs were compared side by side on the brand background. Newsreader won: at light weight it feels airy (like light itself) and its italic is elegant. Hanken Grotesk is warmer than the usual Inter and has tabular figures, digits of equal width, so prices and areas line up.

Optical size (opsz) is a font axis: the letters redraw themselves for large or small use, with finer contrast for headlines. Sizes like "104 → 44 px" are fluid: the text scales smoothly between phone and desktop with CSS clamp().

06 · Image system

Light plates: one way to show every project

Rules for choosing photos

  • Natural daylight only, with a visible direction of light.
  • Eye-level camera, straight vertical lines.
  • Warm neutrals and real materials; at most one strong colour.
  • No people, no logos, five objects in focus at most.
  • Three crops only: 3:2 rooms, 4:5 details, 16:9 hero (4:5 on phones).
  • Every caption names the hour and the light.
3:2
4:5
16:9

The Oriel grade

  • Saturation −12%, brightness +2%
  • Blacks lifted to 12/255, whites capped at 248 (a soft matte)
  • Highlights warmer: red +5, blue −9
  • Shadows cooler: blue +7

Temporary photos. When the Unsplash key is added, each project gets one photographer's series of a single space, then the same grade and crops. That is what makes three unrelated stock shoots read as one studio's work.

Learning note

Stock photos come from different cameras, lenses and editors. Three things unify them: strict selection rules, a fixed set of crops, and one shared colour grade applied by a script so no image is edited differently.

The grade is a split-tone: warm highlights and cool shadows, the same "warm light, cool shadow" idea as the palette.

Learning note

The order follows how a client decides: promise, proof (work), price, how it works, who you are, doubts, action. Competitors jump from photos straight to "Contact".

Section labels are hours of the day instead of 01, 02, 03. That's a structural device carrying meaning: the brand is about daylight, so the page reads as one day, with backgrounds that slowly darken toward the evening.

09 · Motion & access

Nothing moves unless you do

Motion rules

  • No scroll effects, no parallax, no autoplay.
  • Hover: colour and underline, 150 ms.
  • Photos fade in once as they load, 400 ms.
  • Menu and FAQ open with a 250 ms fade.
  • With "reduce motion" switched on, everything is instant.

Access and speed

  • Keyboard reachable, with a visible slate focus ring.
  • Real alt text on every photo; one H1; labelled form fields.
  • Touch targets at least 44 px.
  • AVIF and WebP images at several sizes; fonts hosted with the site.
  • Under 5 KB of JavaScript.

Learning note

Minimal motion means motion is feedback for something the visitor did, never decoration that plays by itself. It also helps people with motion sensitivity, and the prefers-reduced-motion setting lets them turn it off entirely.
